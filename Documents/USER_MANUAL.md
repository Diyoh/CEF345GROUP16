# BuildRight - User Manual

## Introduction

Welcome to **BuildRight**, the automated monitoring tool for public infrastructure projects in Cameroon. This manual guides you through the features available to different users of the platform.

---

## 1. General Public (Citizens)

_No account required._

### Viewing Projects

1.  **Landing Page**: When you visit the site, you will see a "Hero" section with global statistics (Total Projects, Budget, Status).
2.  **Dashboard**: Scroll down or click "Dashboard" to see a list of projects.
3.  **Filtering**: Use the sidebar to filter projects by **Region** (e.g., Littoral, Centre) or **Status** (Ongoing, Completed).

### Reporting an Issue

1.  Click on any project card to view its **Project Details** page.
2.  Scroll down to the "Citizen Reports & Comments" section.
3.  **Form**:
    - Enter your **Name**.
    - Select your type (**Citizen** or **NGO**).
    - Write your observation in the text box.
    - (Optional) Click **Attach Photos** to upload evidence (Max 4 images).
4.  Click **Post Comment**. Your report will be immediately visible.

---

## 2. Contractors

_Requires a Contractor account._

### Login

1.  Click **Login** in the top navigation bar.
2.  Enter your email and password. (Demo: `contact@btpcameroun.cm` / `password`).

### Managing Projects

1.  Upon login, you are directed to the **Contractor Dashboard**.
2.  You will see only the projects assigned to your company.
3.  **Updating Status**:
    - Click **Update Status** on a project card.
    - Change the **Status** (e.g., from Planned to Ongoing).
    - Update the **Progress %** slider.
    - Update the **Amount Spent** if previously authorized funds have been utilized.
    - **Upload Photos**: Add new progress photos to the project gallery.
4.  **Timeline Updates**:
    - Use the "Add Timeline Update" features to log major milestones (e.g., "Foundation Laid").

---

## 3. Administrators

_Requires an Admin account._

### Dashboard Overview

1.  Login as Admin (Demo: `admin@buildright.cm` / `password`).
2.  The **Admin Dashboard** gives you full control over the platform.

### Project Management

1.  **Create Project**: Click the **+ New Project** button. Fill in the title, budget, region, and assign a contractor from the dropdown list.
2.  **Edit Project**: Click the **Edit** (Pencil) icon on any project row to modify its details or reassign it.
3.  **Delete Project**: Click the **Delete** (Trash) icon to remove a project permanently (Warning: This deletes all associated comments and images).

### User Management

1.  **Access Codes**: Go to the "Access Management" tab.
2.  **Generate Code**: Select a role (e.g., Contractor) and click **Generate Code**.
3.  Share this code with a new user. They can use it on the Login page ("I have an invite code") to create their own account linked to that role.

### Comment Moderation

1.  Navigate to the **Comments** tab.
2.  Review citizen reports. If a report is abusive or spam, click **Delete** to remove it.

---

## 4. Developers

_Special access for system maintainers._

### Developer Dashboard

1.  Login as a Developer (Demo: `dev@buildright.cm` / `password`).
2.  **Team Management**: You can edit the "Meet the Team" page visible to the public. Update names, roles, or bios of team members.
