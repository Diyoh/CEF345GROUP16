Third-Party Services for BuildRight

-Payment Gateways

MTN Cameroon/ Orange Money: Facilitates payments within the platform.

M-Pesa: Used for mobile transactions and donations.

SMS Services

-MTN Cameroon: Utilizes MTN's SMS gateway for messaging services.

-Orange Cameroon: Connects via Orange's SMS API.

-Africastalking: Unifies SMS sending across multiple carriers, including MTN and Orange.

-Twilio: An alternative global provider for SMS services, useful for wider reach.

Email Services

-SendGrid: Handles transactional and notification emails.
-Mailgun: Offers email sending and management with analytics.

Map Services

-Google Maps API: For embedding interactive maps to visualize project locations and updates.
-Mapbox: An alternative for advanced mapping capabilities, ideal for responsive designs.

Data Storage and Management

-Firebase: Provides real-time database, authentication, and storage services.
-AWS S3: For additional storage needs, particularly for larger files.
